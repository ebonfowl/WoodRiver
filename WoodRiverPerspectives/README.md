# Wood River Perspectives
This will serve as a central repository for narrative, journalistic, artistic and other qualitative perspectives on the Wood River valley. News articles, oral histories, and historical photographs are linked here.

## Government Resources
[Blaine County's website](https://www.blainecountyid.gov/364/History-Statistics) provides basic historical information, visitor recommendation, and statistics regarding the county's social setting, geography, and economy.

## In the News
This [article](https://www.hcn.org/issues/issue-215/global-market-squeezes-sheep-ranchers) provides a glimpse of the conditions that have shaped sheep ranching in the Wood River valley during your lifetimes. 

The author of [this article](https://www.idahoednews.org/news/blaine-county-redesigns-its-system-to-boost-latino-student-achievement) identifies one of the central issues that led to the formation of the YMCA Summer Bridge program.

This [feature](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2022/may/03/nowhere-to-go-eviction-of-11-latino-families-sheds) describes how the Ketchum housing crunch we spoke about in class is displacing residents on the lower end of the income spectrum.

## Feature Articles
[Hemingway's Ketchum](https://sunvalleymag.com/articles/hemingways-ketchum) tells the story of Ernest Hemingway's last place of residence and final resting place and his place in the lore of Blaine County.

## Oral Histories
Oral histories describing life in Blaine County crica the early 1900s can be found at the [Community Library Digital Collection](https://rhd.thecommunitylibrary.org/luna/servlet/view/all?sort=identifier). These histories chronicle the lived experiences of the inhabitants of the Wood River valley, drawing upon the accounts of low income shepherds and afluent residents alike. *If the link takes you to a different place on the website, navigate to Collections > Wood River Valley Oral Histories.*

## Historical Photographs
The [Community Library Digital Collection](https://rhd.thecommunitylibrary.org/luna/servlet/view/all?sort=identifier) catalogues several photographic examples of life, travel, recreation and demographics in Blaine County. *If the link takes you to a different place on the website, navigate to Collections > Dorice Taylor Photo Collection. Several additional photo collections can be found in the Collections menu as well.*
